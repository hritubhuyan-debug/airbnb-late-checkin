Late-night check-in, confirmed before she leaves

Meera lands at 1:45 AM with low battery and no signal. This prototype moves check-in from the gate to the journey: the guest shares an arrival window, the host confirms a plan and backup, one reminder an hour before, an offline arrival guide, and a clear way out if she still can’t get in.
